HangarX Support

Help with your account, profile, training content, or app experience.

Account & Sign in with Apple

If you are unable to sign in, first confirm that you are using the same Apple account previously connected to HangarX. If you used Hide My Email, your HangarX account may be associated with an Apple private relay address.

Profile sync

HangarX is designed to restore supported cloud profile information when you sign in on another device using the same linked account. If your profile does not appear correctly, contact support and describe the issue. Do not send passwords, Apple private keys, access tokens, or other secrets.

Profile photo

You can update your profile photo from the profile editing experience. If a photo upload fails, check your connection and try again.

Training & exam content

If you believe a question, answer, translation, or training item is incorrect, include the module or exam name and enough context to identify the item.
